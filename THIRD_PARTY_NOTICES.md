# Third-party notices

Image Crucible loads the official single-threaded `ffmpeg.wasm` packages for local video processing:

- `@ffmpeg/ffmpeg` 0.12.15
- `@ffmpeg/core` 0.12.10

The JavaScript wrapper is maintained by the ffmpeg.wasm project. The distributed core is built from FFmpeg with external codec libraries including x264, x265, libvpx and libopus. Keep the corresponding copyright and license notices with any public or commercial deployment, and review the upstream licensing requirements before redistribution.
